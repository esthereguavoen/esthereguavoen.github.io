02 / FEATURED PROJECT

PROJECT 01

Histopathological Effects of Histidine on Mercury Chloride-Induced Hippocampal Toxicity in Adult Wistar Rats

An experimental biomedical research study examining hippocampal tissue changes following mercury chloride exposure and the potential protective effect of L-histidine.

Biomedical ResearchHistopathologyBiochemistryGraphPad Prism
H&E-stained hippocampal sections
RESEARCH QUESTION

Can L-histidine attenuate structural and biochemical changes associated with mercury chloride-induced hippocampal toxicity?

STUDY DESIGN
  • 25 adult Wistar rats
  • 5 experimental groups, n=5
  • 21-day oral gavage protocol
  • Control, histidine-only, HgCl₂-only and combined treatment groups

02Tissue collection & processing
03H&E staining & microscopy
04Biochemical assessment
05Statistical analysis
METHODS & MEASUREMENTS

From tissue sections to quantitative data.

The study combined histological assessment with measurements of oxidative stress and cholinergic activity to compare outcomes across experimental groups.

MDAMalondialdehyde
SODSuperoxide dismutase

KEY FINDING

Histidine-treated groups showed reduced histological alterations compared with the mercury chloride group.

The thesis reports normal hippocampal architecture in the control and histidine-only groups, marked histological alterations in the mercury chloride group, and reduced alterations in the combined histidine groups.

Statistical analysis was performed in GraphPad Prism using one-way ANOVA with appropriate post-hoc testing; significance was set at p<0.05.
